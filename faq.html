Everything you need to get started with Torah Lock. Still wondering about something? We are here to help.

How does Torah Lock work?

Choose the apps you want to pause, then build your daily prayer flow. Your selected apps stay blocked during your scheduled window until you finish your prayers. Complete your moment, and your apps unlock.

Can I customize my prayers?

Yes. Build your own daily flow with prayers including Modeh Ani, Shema, Amidah, and personalized Tehillim. You can choose and arrange the prayers that fit your practice.

What do the widgets show?

Home Screen widgets bring your daily flow, prayer streak, and Tehillim to your screen. Lock Screen widgets let you keep a verse and your daily progress close at hand. Open the widget setup guide in the app to get started.

What happens on Shabbat?

Torah Lock pauses app blocking for Shabbat and major Jewish holidays, and preserves your streak. Location helps the app use the right local times.

Is Torah Lock free?

Torah Lock is free to download, with in-app subscription options. The app shows the current price and any available trial before you subscribe. Manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone’s Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Can Torah Lock see which apps I use?

App selection and blocking happen on your iPhone through Apple’s Screen Time framework. Torah Lock does not receive your selected app list or personal app usage. The app uses analytics and other services described in our Privacy Policy.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Torah Lock, choose a widget, and tap Add Widget. For Lock Screen widgets, touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, and tap the widget area. Open Torah Lock at least once before adding it.

Is there an Android version?

This website currently links to Torah Lock for iPhone. For questions about other platforms, email support@torahlock.app.
