1. Use of the App

Torah Lock is an iOS application designed to help users begin their day with a short spiritual moment — including prayer (Shema) and Tehillim (Psalms) — before accessing selected apps.

The app uses Apple's FamilyControls and ManagedSettings frameworks to temporarily restrict selected applications during a morning enforcement window (default: 5:30 AM – 12:00 PM) until the daily spiritual session is completed.

You agree to use Torah Lock only for its intended personal and spiritual use. Any attempt to bypass, manipulate, or interfere with the app's restriction features outside of the provided emergency unlock is prohibited.

2. Local Data & No Account Requirement

Torah Lock does not require you to create an account.

All personal progress data (such as streaks, check-in scores, preferences, and session history) is stored locally on your device. We do not monitor or track your personal device activity outside the app.

During onboarding, you may provide your first name, age range, preferences, and optionally a profile photo. This information is stored locally on your device. Your first name is also sent to our analytics provider (PostHog) to personalize your experience. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

Torah Lock offers optional paid features through auto-renewable subscriptions.

  • Payments are processed exclusively through Apple's App Store.
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID settings (Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions).
  • Refunds are handled according to Apple's App Store refund policies.
  • RevenueCat is used to manage subscription entitlements and verify purchase status.

4. Device Restrictions (Family Controls)

App restrictions are performed locally on your device using Apple's FamilyControls and ManagedSettings system frameworks.

  • You select which apps to restrict. This selection is stored only on your device.
  • Torah Lock does not collect, transmit, or store which apps you selected or how you use them.
  • Restrictions are automatically removed when the enforcement window ends (12:00 PM) or when you complete the daily moment.
  • An emergency unlock feature is available to temporarily remove restrictions before completion. Use of this feature is logged as an anonymous analytics event.

The restriction functionality is intended solely for voluntary personal self-management. Torah Lock is not a parental control app.

Data shared with third-party services is limited to what is necessary for their function. No data is used for advertising or sold to third parties. Each service operates under its own privacy policy and terms.

Your coordinates and timezone are sent to the Hebcal API to retrieve Shabbat times, which are then cached locally on your device. You may deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.
