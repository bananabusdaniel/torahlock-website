Open Torah Lock

Download the app, open it at least once, and set up your daily flow. Then return to your Home Screen.

Add your Home Screen widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Torah Lock, choose your widget, and tap Add Widget.

Make room on your Lock Screen

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, and choose Lock Screen. Tap the widget area and select Torah Lock.

Need a hand? The app includes a widget setup guide under Profile. Or get in touch.
